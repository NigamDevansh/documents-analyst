1. Parties and Background

This Amendment No. 1 to the Master Services Agreement dated 1 January 2025, between Acme Corporation and Beta Logistics LLC, is entered into between Acme Corporation ("Customer") and Beta Logistics LLC ("Supplier"). This Amendment modifies certain terms of the Master Services Agreement as set forth below. All capitalized terms not defined herein shall have the meanings assigned to them in the Master Services Agreement.

2. Effective Date

This amendment is effective as of 1 April 2025. From and after the effective date, the terms of this Amendment shall supersede any conflicting terms in the Master Services Agreement.

3. Amendment to Monthly Fee

Section 4.2 of the Agreement is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following: the monthly fee shall be USD 12,000. This revised monthly fee shall apply to all invoices issued on or after the effective date of this Amendment.

4. Amendment to Payment Terms

Section 5.1 of the Agreement is hereby deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following: all fees are due Net 45. Customer shall make all payments within forty-five days of the invoice date. This change to payment terms shall apply to all outstanding and future invoices.

5. General Provisions

Except as expressly modified herein, all terms of the Master Services Agreement remain in full force and effect. This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. This Amendment constitutes the entire agreement of the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior negotiations and understandings.
