1. Parties and Background

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into between Acme Corporation ("Customer") and Beta Logistics LLC ("Supplier") pursuant to the Master Services Agreement between the parties dated 1 January 2025. This SOW describes the specific services, fees, and deliverables for the northeast distribution network project.

2. Term

This Statement of Work is effective as of 1 January 2025. The services hereunder shall continue through the expiry date of the underlying Agreement, and this Statement of Work shall remain in effect until 31 December 2027.

3. Scope of Services

Supplier shall provide dedicated freight coordination services for Customer's northeast distribution network. The services include route planning for up to fifty delivery routes per week, warehouse scheduling, carrier negotiation, and real-time shipment tracking. Supplier shall assign a dedicated account manager to oversee operations and shall provide weekly performance reports. Customer shall provide accurate shipment forecasts and timely access to loading facilities.

4. Fees and Payment

The monthly fee for the services described in Section 3 shall be USD 10,000. The fees shall be billed monthly in arrears based on services rendered. The total contract value of this Statement of Work is USD 360,000. Invoicing shall commence on the effective date. Customer acknowledges that the total value represents the full consideration for the services during the term, excluding pass-through costs for third-party carriers.

5. Deliverables and Acceptance

Supplier shall complete the initial route optimization within forty-five days of the effective date. Customer shall have ten business days to review and accept the proposed routes. Failure to object within the inspection period shall constitute acceptance. Ongoing deliverables include weekly status reports and monthly cost analyses.

6. General Provisions

This Statement of Work may not be amended except in writing signed by authorized representatives of both parties. In the event of conflict between this SOW and the Master Services Agreement, the Master Services Agreement shall control. All undefined terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Master Services Agreement.
