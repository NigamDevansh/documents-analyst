1. Parties and Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Cobalt Manufacturing Inc ("Customer") and Sparrow Industrial Supply Co ("Supplier"). Customer is a Texas corporation with its principal place of business at 300 Factory Road, Houston, Texas. Supplier is a Texas corporation with its principal place of business at 400 Warehouse Blvd, Dallas, Texas. This Agreement governs the provision of industrial supply procurement and inventory management services by Supplier to Customer.

2. Term

This agreement is effective as of 1 February 2025. The initial term of this Agreement shall be for a period of twelve (12) months. The term shall commence on the effective date and continue through the anniversary date unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

3. Renewal and Termination

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless either party provides written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately for uncured material breach. Upon termination, Customer shall pay all fees for services rendered through the termination date.

4. Services

Supplier shall provide industrial supply procurement, vendor management, and inventory tracking services as described in Exhibit A. Supplier shall maintain adequate stock levels of critical components as specified in the inventory schedule. Supplier shall deliver ordered items to Customer's designated facilities within the timeframes set forth in Exhibit B.

5. Fees and Payment

The monthly fee for the services described in Section 4 shall be USD 5,000. All fees are due Net 60. Customer shall pay each invoice within the stated period.

6. Indemnification

Supplier shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims arising from Supplier's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement. Customer shall promptly notify Supplier of any claim and cooperate in the defense.

7. Warranties

Supplier warrants that all supplies provided shall conform to the specifications set forth in the purchase orders for a period of ninety days from delivery.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
