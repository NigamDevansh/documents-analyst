DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

1. Parties and Scope

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") is entered into between Meridian Health Systems LLC ("Controller") and CareBridge Analytics Inc ("Processor") and forms part of the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated 1 April 2025. This DPA applies to all processing of Customer Personal Data carried out by Processor on behalf of Controller.

2. Effective Date

This DPA is effective as of 1 April 2025.

3. Roles of the Parties

Controller determines the purposes and means of the processing of Customer Personal Data. Processor processes Customer Personal Data only on the documented instructions of Controller, including with regard to transfers of Customer Personal Data to a third country.

4. Categories of Data

The Customer Personal Data processed under this DPA comprises patient demographic identifiers, encounter records, diagnosis and procedure codes, laboratory results and clinician identifiers.

5. Subprocessors

Processor may engage subprocessors to process Customer Personal Data. Processor shall maintain an up-to-date list of subprocessors and shall give Controller not less than thirty (30) days written notice before appointing any new subprocessor. Processor remains fully liable to Controller for the performance of each subprocessor's obligations.

6. Security Measures

Processor shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit and at rest, role-based access control, logging of administrative access, and annual penetration testing by an independent third party.

7. Personnel

Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to binding confidentiality obligations and have received appropriate training in the handling of protected health information.

8. Breach Notification

Processor shall notify Controller without undue delay, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

9. Audit Rights

Processor shall make available to Controller all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and shall allow for and contribute to audits conducted by Controller or an auditor mandated by Controller, not more than once in any twelve month period.

10. Return and Deletion

Upon expiry or termination of the Master Services Agreement, Processor shall, at the election of Controller, return or securely delete all Customer Personal Data within ninety (90) days, save to the extent retention is required by applicable law.

11. Confidentiality

The confidentiality obligations in this DPA shall survive for a period of sixty (60) months following expiry or termination.
