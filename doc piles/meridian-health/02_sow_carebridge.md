STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

1. Parties and Incorporation

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is entered into between Meridian Health Systems LLC ("Customer") and CareBridge Analytics Inc ("Supplier") and is governed by the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated 1 April 2025. Capitalised terms not defined in this SOW have the meaning given to them in the Master Services Agreement.

2. Effective Date

This SOW is effective as of 15 April 2025.

3. Scope of Work

Supplier shall perform the following implementation services:

Phase one covers discovery and data mapping across Customer's electronic health record systems, including source system inventory, field-level mapping and an agreed data dictionary. Phase two covers build and configuration of the ingestion pipelines, the cohort reporting library and the administrative console. Phase three covers user acceptance testing, cutover support and two weeks of hypercare following go-live.

4. Deliverables

Supplier shall deliver a data mapping specification, a configured production tenant, a validated cohort reporting library covering the twelve agreed clinical measures, an administrator runbook and a completed user acceptance test report.

5. Fees and Payment

The professional services fees for the work described in this SOW are USD 185,000, invoiced on completion of each phase. All invoices issued under this Statement of Work are payable Net 45 from the date of invoice.

6. Service Levels

Supplier shall maintain platform availability of not less than 99.5% measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least five business days in advance. Supplier shall acknowledge any priority one incident within four (4) hours.

7. Data storage location

All Customer Personal Data ingested under this Statement of Work shall reside in United States regions only. Supplier shall notify Customer in writing before provisioning any new processing region.

8. Acceptance

The acceptance period for each deliverable runs for three working weeks from delivery, during which Customer may test the deliverable and either accept it or provide written notice of non-conformity. A deliverable not rejected within the acceptance period is deemed accepted.

9. Key Personnel

Supplier shall assign a named engagement lead and a named clinical data architect to this SOW. Supplier shall not replace either individual without Customer's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

10. Change Control

Any change to the scope, fees or timeline recorded in this SOW must be documented in a written change order signed by an authorised representative of each Party.
