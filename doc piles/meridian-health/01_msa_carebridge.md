MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 April 2025 by and between Meridian Health Systems LLC ("Customer"), a limited liability company organised under the laws of the State of Illinois with its principal place of business at 400 North Wacker Drive, Chicago, Illinois, and CareBridge Analytics Inc ("Supplier"), a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 88 Harbour Point Road, Boston, Massachusetts. Customer and Supplier are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

2. Purpose and Recitals

Customer operates a network of regional hospitals and outpatient clinics. Supplier provides a hosted clinical analytics platform together with related implementation, configuration and support services. The Parties wish to record the terms on which Supplier will make that platform available to Customer and its Affiliates.

3. Term

This Agreement is effective as of 1 April 2025. The initial term shall be thirty-six (36) months from the effective date. This Agreement shall expire on 31 March 2028 unless renewed or terminated in accordance with its terms.

4. Renewal

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months. Either Party may give written notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

5. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other Party. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party commits a material breach that remains uncured thirty (30) days after notice of that breach. The rights of termination in this Section are available to both Parties on equivalent terms.

6. Services

Supplier shall provide the hosted clinical analytics platform described in each Statement of Work, including data ingestion pipelines, cohort reporting, scheduled extracts and the administrative console. Supplier shall perform all services in a professional and workmanlike manner and in accordance with generally accepted industry standards for clinical software.

7. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay a recurring subscription fee of USD 40,000 per month. Fees are invoiced monthly in advance. All invoices are payable Net 30 from the date of invoice. Amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.

8. Limitation of Liability

The total aggregate liability of CareBridge Analytics Inc arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed USD 480,000. This limitation applies regardless of the form of action, whether in contract, tort, negligence, strict liability or otherwise, and applies in the aggregate across all claims.

9. Indemnification

CareBridge Analytics Inc shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Meridian Health Systems LLC against any third-party claim alleging that the platform infringes a United States patent, copyright or trade secret. Customer shall promptly notify Supplier of any such claim and shall provide reasonable cooperation in the defence.

10. Insurance

CareBridge Analytics Inc shall maintain, at its own expense, commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than USD 5,000,000 per occurrence, together with technology errors and omissions coverage. Certificates of insurance shall be furnished upon Customer's reasonable request.

11. Confidentiality

Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party except as permitted under this Agreement. The obligations in this Section shall survive for a period of sixty (60) months following expiry or termination. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party.

12. Data Protection

The Data Processing Addendum executed by the Parties is incorporated into this Agreement by reference. Supplier may engage subprocessors to process Customer Personal Data provided that Supplier remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of each subprocessor.

13. Data residency region

All Customer Personal Data processed under this Agreement shall be stored and processed exclusively within data centres located in the United States. Supplier shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the United States without the prior written consent of Customer.

14. Service Credits

Supplier shall issue service credits against the next monthly invoice where the platform fails to meet the availability commitments recorded in the applicable Statement of Work.

15. Support Response

Supplier shall acknowledge any priority one incident reported by Customer within four (4) hours of receipt.

16. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
