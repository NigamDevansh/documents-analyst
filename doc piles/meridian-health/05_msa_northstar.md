MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 June 2025 by and between Meridian Health Systems LLC ("Customer"), a limited liability company organised under the laws of the State of Illinois, and Northstar Imaging Partners LLC ("Supplier"), a limited liability company organised under the laws of the State of Minnesota with its principal place of business at 2200 Lakeview Parkway, Minneapolis, Minnesota.

2. Purpose

Supplier provides teleradiology reporting services, including overnight preliminary reads and subspecialty final reads, to hospital networks. Customer wishes to engage Supplier to provide those services across its regional facilities.

3. Term

This Agreement is effective as of 1 June 2025. The initial term shall be twenty-four (24) months and this Agreement shall expire on 31 May 2027.

4. Renewal

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. Termination

Northstar Imaging Partners LLC may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time upon thirty (30) days prior written notice to Customer. Customer may terminate this Agreement only in the event of a material breach by Supplier that remains uncured sixty (60) days after written notice of that breach.

6. Services

Supplier shall provide teleradiology interpretation services on a twenty-four hour basis, including preliminary reads returned within sixty minutes of study transmission and final subspecialty reads returned within twelve hours.

7. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay a recurring service fee of USD 62,000 per month. All invoices issued under this Agreement are payable Net 60 from the date of invoice.

8. Warranties

Supplier warrants that all interpreting physicians are board certified, hold unrestricted licences in the jurisdictions in which Customer operates, and are credentialled in accordance with Customer's medical staff bylaws.

9. Insurance

Supplier shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than USD 3,000,000 per claim covering all interpreting physicians performing services under this Agreement.

10. Confidentiality

Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in strict confidence. The obligations in this Section shall survive for a period of thirty-six (36) months following expiry or termination.

11. Data Protection

Supplier shall comply with all applicable requirements of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act in its handling of protected health information received from Customer.

12. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Minnesota.
