AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Parties

This Amendment No. 1 (this "Amendment") is entered into between Meridian Health Systems LLC ("Customer") and CareBridge Analytics Inc ("Supplier") and amends the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated 1 April 2025 (the "Original Agreement").

2. Effective Date

This Amendment is effective as of 1 October 2025.

3. Background

Following the addition of three regional hospitals to Customer's network, the Parties wish to expand the licensed scope of the platform and to record the revised subscription fee that applies to the expanded deployment.

4. Amendment to Fees

Section 7 of the Original Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following: "Customer shall pay a recurring subscription fee of USD 55,000 per month. Fees are invoiced monthly in advance. All invoices are payable Net 30 from the date of invoice."

5. Expanded Scope

The licensed scope is extended to cover the three additional hospital sites listed in Schedule 1 to this Amendment, together with their affiliated outpatient clinics.

6. No Other Changes

Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, all other terms and conditions of the Original Agreement, including the limitation of liability in Section 8, remain in full force and effect and are hereby ratified and confirmed.

7. Governing Law

This Amendment shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
