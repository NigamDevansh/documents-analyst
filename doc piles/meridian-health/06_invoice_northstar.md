INVOICE AND STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT

1. Parties

This statement of account is issued by Northstar Imaging Partners LLC ("Supplier") to Meridian Health Systems LLC ("Customer") under the Master Services Agreement between the Parties dated 1 June 2025.

2. Invoice Details

Invoice number NSI-2025-0114 is dated 1 November 2025 and covers teleradiology services rendered during the month of October 2025.

3. Charges

The recurring service fee for the period is USD 62,000. Additional subspecialty final reads beyond the contracted volume are charged at USD 145 per study, and 212 such studies were performed during the period, giving additional charges of USD 30,740.

4. Payment Terms

The total amount due is USD 92,740. This invoice is payable Net 45 from the date of invoice. Remittance should be made to the account recorded in Schedule 2 of the Master Services Agreement.

5. Late Payment

Amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.0% per month on the outstanding balance.

6. Queries

Any query in respect of this statement must be raised in writing within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.
