1. Parties and Background

This Statement of Work is entered into between Northwind Trading Ltd ("Customer") and Vertex Cloud Systems Inc ("Supplier") pursuant to the Master Services Agreement between the parties effective 1 March 2025. This Statement of Work describes the specific services, fees, and deliverables for the dedicated hosting project.

2. Term

This Statement of Work is effective as of 1 March 2025. The services hereunder shall continue through the expiry date of the underlying Agreement, and this Statement of Work shall expire on 28 February 2027.

3. Scope of Services

Supplier shall provide dedicated cloud hosting services for Customer's e-commerce platform. The services include provisioning of virtual machine instances, managed database clusters, application load balancers, and automated daily backup services. Supplier shall monitor the infrastructure twenty-four hours per day and apply security patches as they become available. Customer shall provide timely access to its systems and cooperate with Supplier's reasonable technical requests.

4. Fees and Payment

The monthly fee for the services described in Section 3 shall be USD 20,000. The fees shall be billed monthly in arrears. The total contract value of this Statement of Work is USD 480,000. Invoicing shall commence on the effective date. Customer acknowledges that the total value represents the full consideration for the services during the term.

5. Deliverables and Acceptance

Supplier shall deliver the configured environment within thirty days of the effective date. Customer shall have ten business days to inspect and accept the deliverables. Failure to object within the inspection period shall constitute acceptance.

6. General Provisions

This Statement of Work may not be amended except in writing signed by both parties. In the event of conflict between this Statement of Work and the Master Services Agreement, the Master Services Agreement shall control. All undefined terms shall have the meanings ascribed to them in the Master Services Agreement.
