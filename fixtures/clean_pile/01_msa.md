1. Parties and Agreement

This Master Services Agreement is entered into between Northwind Trading Ltd ("Customer") and Vertex Cloud Systems Inc ("Supplier"). Customer is a company incorporated under the laws of England with its principal place of business at 123 Thames Street, London. Supplier is a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 456 Silicon Avenue, San Jose, California. This Agreement sets forth the terms and conditions governing the provision of cloud infrastructure services by Supplier to Customer.

2. Term

This agreement is effective as of 1 March 2025. The initial term of this Agreement shall be for a period of twenty-four (24) months. This Agreement shall expire on 28 February 2027.

3. Renewal

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods. Either party may provide written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

4. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days written notice.

5. Services

During the term, Supplier shall provide the cloud infrastructure services described in Exhibit A, including compute resources, managed storage, network connectivity, and automated backup services. Supplier shall perform the services in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with industry standards.

6. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay all fees set forth in applicable Statements of Work. All fees are due Net 30. In the event of late payment, Supplier may suspend services after providing ten days' notice.

7. Limitation of Liability

Vertex Cloud Systems Inc's total liability shall not exceed USD 240,000. The liability cap is calculated as twelve (12) months of fees. This limitation applies regardless of the form of action, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise.

8. Indemnification

Vertex Cloud Systems Inc shall indemnify Northwind Trading Ltd against third-party claims arising from the performance of the services. Customer shall promptly notify Supplier of any claim and cooperate in the defense.

9. Warranties

Vertex Cloud Systems Inc warrants that the services shall conform to the specifications for a warranty period of twelve (12) months. Supplier does not warrant that the services will be uninterrupted or error-free, but shall use commercially reasonable efforts to correct material defects.

10. Insurance

Vertex Cloud Systems Inc shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of USD 2,000,000. Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance upon Customer's reasonable request.

11. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to hold the other party's confidential information in strict confidence. The confidentiality obligations shall survive for a period of thirty-six (36) months following termination. Confidential information does not include information that is publicly available or independently developed.

12. Data Protection

The Data Processing Addendum attached hereto as Schedule A is incorporated by reference. Vertex Cloud Systems Inc may engage subprocessors subject to the terms of this Agreement, provided that Supplier remains liable for the acts of its subprocessors.

13. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
