1. Parties and Purpose

This Service Level Agreement is entered into between Northwind Trading Ltd and Vertex Cloud Systems Inc. This Agreement supplements the Master Services Agreement and establishes the specific service levels, support obligations, and remedies applicable to the cloud infrastructure services provided by Supplier.

2. Uptime Commitment

Supplier commits to maintaining 99.9% monthly uptime for the production environment. Uptime is calculated based on the percentage of minutes in a calendar month during which the production services are available and operational, excluding scheduled maintenance windows and force majeure events. Scheduled maintenance shall not exceed four hours per month and shall be conducted during off-peak hours with at least seventy-two hours advance notice.

3. Support Response

Supplier shall provide technical support twenty-four hours per day, seven days per week, through its dedicated support portal and telephone hotline. For critical incidents affecting production systems, Supplier shall respond within four (4) hours of receiving a properly submitted support ticket. For non-critical incidents, Supplier shall respond within one business day. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to resolve critical incidents within twenty-four hours.

4. Service Credits

A service credits clause shall apply for any month in which the uptime commitment is not achieved. If the monthly uptime falls below 99.9% but remains at or above 99.0%, Customer shall receive a credit equal to 5% of the monthly fee. If the monthly uptime falls below 99.0%, Customer shall receive a credit equal to 10% of the monthly fee. Service credits shall be applied against future invoices and shall constitute the sole and exclusive remedy for service level failures.

5. Monitoring and Reporting

Supplier shall monitor the services using industry-standard monitoring tools and shall provide monthly uptime reports within five business days of the end of each calendar month. Each report shall include details of any incidents, their duration, and root cause analysis. Customer may request additional performance data on a quarterly basis.

6. Review and Improvement

The parties shall meet quarterly to review service level performance, discuss trends, and agree on improvement plans where performance consistently fails to meet targets. Either party may request an ad-hoc review meeting upon thirty days written notice.

7. General

This Service Level Agreement is incorporated into and governed by the Master Services Agreement. In case of conflict, the terms of the Master Services Agreement shall prevail.
