1. Services

The supplier shall provide managed IT support services as described in the scope document attached hereto as Exhibit A. The services include remote monitoring of servers and workstations, automated patch management, antivirus and endpoint protection administration, help desk support during standard business hours, and quarterly system health reviews. The supplier shall assign qualified technical personnel to perform the services and shall maintain appropriate certifications. The supplier shall perform the services in a professional manner consistent with industry standards and best practices.

2. Fees and Payment

The monthly fee for the services described in Section 1 shall be USD 3,000. The fees shall be billed monthly in advance on the first day of each month. Payment shall be due within thirty days of the invoice date. Late payments may result in suspension of non-critical services after ten days' written notice. The customer shall remain responsible for all fees accrued through the date of termination.

3. Term

The initial term shall be for a period of six (6) months. The term shall commence on the effective date specified in the applicable agreement between the parties.

4. Renewal

This agreement shall automatically renew for successive six-month periods unless either party provides written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

5. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement upon thirty (30) days written notice. Either party may terminate this agreement immediately for uncured material breach. Upon termination, the customer shall return all confidential information and materials belonging to the supplier.

6. Limitation of Liability

The supplier's total liability shall not exceed USD 50,000. The liability cap represents a fixed amount. This limitation applies regardless of the form of action, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, and regardless of whether the remedy arises at law or in equity.

7. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to hold the other party's confidential information in strict confidence and to use such information solely for the purpose of exercising rights and performing obligations under this agreement. The confidentiality obligations shall survive for a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination. Confidential information does not include information that is publicly available or rightfully obtained from third parties without restriction.

8. Data Protection

The supplier shall implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction. The supplier shall notify the customer of any security incident affecting customer data within forty-eight hours of discovery.

9. Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
