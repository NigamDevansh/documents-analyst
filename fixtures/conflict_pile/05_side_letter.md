Re: Master Services Agreement between Acme Corporation and Beta Logistics LLC

This side letter ("Side Letter") is entered into between Acme Corporation ("Customer") and Beta Logistics LLC ("Supplier") to supplement and modify certain terms of the Master Services Agreement dated 1 January 2025 between the parties. This Side Letter is intended to address specific operational requirements that have arisen since the execution of the Master Services Agreement.

1. Expiry Date

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Master Services Agreement, the parties agree that the agreement expires on 30 June 2027. This revised expiry date reflects the parties' mutual agreement to conclude the engagement earlier than originally contemplated due to changes in Customer's business requirements. All obligations that by their nature should survive termination, including confidentiality and indemnification, shall survive in accordance with the Master Services Agreement.

2. Data Residency

All customer data to be stored in the Frankfurt region. Supplier shall ensure that no customer data is processed, stored, or transmitted outside the Frankfurt region without Customer's prior written consent. Supplier shall implement technical and organizational measures to enforce this restriction and shall provide annual compliance certifications to Customer. This requirement applies to all subprocessors engaged by Supplier.

3. Cooperation and Audit

Supplier shall cooperate with Customer's reasonable requests to verify data residency compliance. Customer may conduct up to one audit per calendar year upon thirty days advance written notice. Supplier shall provide access to relevant documentation and system logs to confirm that customer data remains within the Frankfurt region.

4. General Provisions

This Side Letter shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. In the event of conflict between this Side Letter and the Master Services Agreement, the terms of this Side Letter shall prevail. This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts and may only be amended by mutual written agreement of the parties.
